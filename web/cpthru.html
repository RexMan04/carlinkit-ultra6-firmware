Pass-through

On sends your iPhone's video straight to the car. Off uses the adapter's stock re-encoding. Applies the next time CarPlay connects. Tap twice to change.

Audio delay

How much audio the adapter buffers before playing. Lower feels snappier but can crackle or drop out when Wi-Fi hiccups. "Phone decides" is the default. 300 ms is the lowest fixed value the firmware accepts (the stock menu stops at 400). Applies the next time CarPlay connects. Tap twice to change.
